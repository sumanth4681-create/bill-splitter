How the scope grew

Scope said "people claim their items." Now we know exactly what that means: friends' names go above the items, a person dropdown picks who you're claiming for, selecting an item puts that name under it, and selecting it under two people makes it shared. Unclaimed items, tax, and tip are split equally, and final amounts can be edited.

Features and acceptance criteria

Receipt upload and reading

Photo → Google OCR → items, each with a unique ID, name, quantity, and price. Tax and tip are captured separately.

  • A real receipt photo shows the spinner, then items matching the receipt
  • Two same-name items appear as two separate rows, claimable independently
Fixing misreads

Quantity and price are editable on each row, only to correct OCR errors.

  • Editing a price updates the summary immediately
People

Friends' names are entered above the items and populate the person dropdown.

  • Adding three names puts all three in the dropdown
Claiming items: the kernel

Pick a person, then select items. Their name appears under each item. Selecting an item under 2+ people makes it shared among only them. Deselecting removes the claim.

  • Nachos selected as A, then B: both names shown, and C pays nothing for it
  • Deselecting removes the name and updates the summary
Split summary

Per person: name, items, and the amount owed to the payer. Unclaimed items, tax, and tip are split equally by default, and final amounts are editable.

  • All amounts sum to the receipt total (before manual edits)
  • Unclaimed items are labeled and spread across everyone
  • Editing a final amount shows the new amount
History

Every processed bill is saved and listed by name. Reopening it restores the items, names, claims, and summary.

  • A processed bill appears in History, and reopening it shows the same claims

Boundary

Building now

Upload + spinner → Google OCR → editable items with unique IDs → names + dropdown → claim/share → summary (equal splits, editable) → History.

Deferred
  • Shareable link and multiple devices (architecture designed in spec, not built)
  • Accept / recalculate / approval rounds / publish
  • Claiming by quantity (e.g., 3 beers split 2-and-1)
Non-goals
  • In-app payments
  • Accounts or login

Open questions

  • Bill name: assumed to default to the restaurant name or date, and editable
  • Summary placement: assumed below the names, above the items
  • Rounding: who gets the extra cent? Can be decided in spec
